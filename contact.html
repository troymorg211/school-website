Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission?

You can apply online through our admissions page or visit our office to pick up an application form. The process involves filling out forms, submitting documents, and an entrance assessment.

What are the school fees?

Fees vary by grade level. Please visit our admissions page for detailed fee structure. We also offer sibling discounts and flexible payment plans.

Do you offer boarding facilities?

Yes, we offer boarding facilities for secondary school students (Grade 10-12). The boarding program includes supervised study sessions and weekend activities.

Is transport provided?

Yes, we have a fleet of school buses that cover various routes in Nairobi and surrounding areas. Contact our office for specific routes and fees.

Can I schedule a school tour?

Absolutely! We welcome prospective parents for school tours. Please contact us to schedule a visit during school days. Tours are conducted on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:00 AM.

What curriculum do you follow?

We follow the Kenyan Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) from pre-primary through secondary level, preparing students for KCSE examinations.
