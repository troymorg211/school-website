# Bright Future Academy design

Direction: welcoming, photographic school website that builds on the original blue-and-gold identity. The green, photo-free iteration is superseded by SCHOOL-DESIGN-BRIEF.md at the user's request.
Dials: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

- School blue establishes the identity and separates navigation from records.
- White and quiet blue neutrals keep school content and portal records readable.
- Restrained gold highlights admissions and the next primary action; dark text preserves contrast.
- Locally hosted Poppins restores the original approachable school identity without runtime font-provider requests.
- Public content uses varied editorial sections because admissions, news, and curriculum need different reading structures.
- Portal navigation follows each role's tasks; records use tables because people compare statuses, dates, and amounts.
- Spacing groups related fields and separates primary decisions from supporting information.
- Panels group a single task or record set; cards are not used as interchangeable marketing filler.
- Relevant locally hosted stock photographs illustrate classroom learning, early years, reading and sport; they are clearly identified as placeholders for a fictional school.
- WhatsApp remains a visible contact entry point with a functioning sample enquiry flow. No real message is sent or live school number invented.
- Small radii distinguish inputs from page sections; focus outlines mark keyboard actions.
- Motion is limited to interaction feedback and follows reduced-motion preferences.
- Public pages lead with school life, learning stages, campus and admissions. Demo implementation details belong in the footer and documentation.

Apply antislop core, antislop-ui and relevant frontend design skills throughout every build, during planning and implementation. Saved mode is during. No skill mandates green; user preferences take precedence.

Research references: [International School of Kenya](https://www.isk.ac.ke/), [Makini Schools](https://www.makinischool.ac.ke/), and [Braeburn](https://braeburn.com/). School-first hierarchy informed this refinement; their branding, claims and photographs are not copied.
