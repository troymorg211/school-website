Before your visit

What should we ask about?

Ask about class routines, learning support, homework, school meals, transport and how teachers communicate with families.

What documents might a school request?

A real admissions office may request identity and age documents, previous school reports and relevant support information. Do not upload personal documents to this demo.

Can we explore the parent portal?

Yes. Open the school portal and select the parent role to see linked children's learning, sample fees and resources.
