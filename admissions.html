Welcome to Admissions

We're delighted that you're considering Bright Future Academy for your child's education. Our admissions process is designed to help us understand each student's unique potential and ensure we can provide the best possible educational experience.

Application Process

1

Inquiry & Visit

Submit an inquiry form or schedule a school tour to learn more about our programs and facilities.

2

Application

Complete the application form and submit required documents including birth certificate and previous reports.

3

Assessment

Student will undergo an entrance assessment to determine appropriate grade placement.

4

Interview

Parents and student will meet with our admissions team for an interview.

5

Decision

Successful applicants will receive an admission letter within 2 weeks.

6

Enrollment

Complete fee payment and submit enrollment documents to secure your place.

Admission Requirements

Pre-Primary

  • Child must be 3+ years
  • Birth Certificate
  • Immunization Records
  • 2 Passport Photos

Primary (Grade 1-6)

  • Age-appropriate for grade
  • Previous School Reports
  • Transfer Letter (if applicable)
  • Entrance Assessment

Secondary (Grade 7-12)

  • KCPE/KPSEA Results
  • Character Reference
  • Medical Certificate
  • Parent Interview

Additional Information

  • Fees are payable per term (3 terms per academic year)
  • Boarding option available for Secondary students at additional KSh 25,000 per term
  • Sibling discount: 10% off for second child, 15% for third child
  • Lunch service available at KSh 3,000 per term (optional)
  • Transport service available - contact for rates based on location
