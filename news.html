Creative learning · 25 September 2026

Little artists, big ideas

A classroom exhibition is a chance for children to explain their thinking as well as show their artwork. In this sample school story, learners choose a drawing, describe the details they included and listen to one another's ideas.

Teachers connect the activity with storytelling, observation and language. Families are encouraged to ask open questions: What is happening in your picture? Which part did you enjoy creating? What would you try next?

School community · 18 September 2026

A whole school of readers

Reading grows through small, regular moments. Our sample reading circle invites learners to bring a favourite story, explain why they chose it and recommend a book to a classmate.

Families can join the conversation at home by reading together and allowing children to choose books that interest them. The aim is simple: make stories a shared pleasure, alongside the skills that build confident readers.

Beyond the classroom · 11 September 2026

Learning to play as a team

Sport offers a practical lesson in communication, persistence and fair play. In this fictional programme, learners practise passing, take turns leading a warm-up and reflect on how the team worked together.

Success includes the player who encourages a teammate, follows the rules and returns to practise a difficult skill. Those habits matter well beyond the court.
