15 January 2026

Students Win Regional Science Fair

Admin Academic Achievement

Our talented Grade 10 students, Amanda Wanjiku and Brian Mutua, took home first place at the Regional Science Fair with their innovative project on renewable energy solutions using solar-powered water purification systems.

The project impressed judges with its practical application in addressing water scarcity issues in rural communities. The students will now represent our region at the National Science Congress scheduled for March.

08 January 2026

Football Team Advances to Semi-Finals

Sports Dept Sports

Bright Future Academy's football team secured a thrilling 2-1 victory against Westlands High School, advancing to the semi-finals of the Inter-School Football Tournament.

Captain Michael Omondi scored the winning goal in the 85th minute, sending the crowd into a frenzy. The team will face St. Mary's Academy in the semi-finals next Saturday at the County Stadium.

02 January 2026

New Library Wing Officially Opened

Admin Infrastructure

We're excited to announce the official opening of our expanded library facility. The new wing features over 5,000 new books, a digital resource center, quiet study areas, and a multimedia section.

The facility was commissioned by the County Education Officer, who praised our commitment to fostering a culture of reading and research among students.

20 December 2025

Choir Wins Gold at National Music Festival

Arts Dept Arts & Culture

Our school choir delivered an outstanding performance at the National Music Festival, earning a Gold Award in the Secondary School Category.

Under the direction of Mr. David Kariuki, the choir performed a blend of traditional African songs and contemporary pieces that captivated the audience and judges alike.
