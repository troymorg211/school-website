School design references were inspected for navigation, admissions hierarchy and school storytelling: [International School of Kenya](https://www.isk.ac.ke/) and [Makini Schools](https://www.makinischool.ac.ke/). Their branding, copy and photographs are not reused.

The visible WhatsApp entry point opens an admissions-contact demonstration with useful enquiry topics. No live WhatsApp number or real message sending is enabled.
